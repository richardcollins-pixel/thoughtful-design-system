# Component catalog

Generated by `skills/build_catalog.py` from every component’s `meta.json`. Do not edit. The `meta.json` (in `tds/<tier>/<name>/`) is the full source: markup or template, items, slots, overrides.

Read the tiers in order: a component is only built from components in tiers above it in this list.

## Atoms

### `atoms/blob` — Blob
The animated shape that is T. It bounces slowly up and down, its outline morphs slowly, and its colors travel round the edge while the theme shifts every few seconds (cool, warm, rose). In special cases it becomes another shape to stand for what T is doing (a pencil, a chat bubble, an email); the plain blob is its default, and it morphs back.
- Props: shape (blob | pencil | bubble | envelope | chat), theme (cool | warm | rose | ember | bloom), cycle (on | off), loop (off | pencil | envelope | bubble | chat), size (24 | 32 | 48 | 80 | 124 | 180)
- Figma: https://www.figma.com/design/pdq8KcTCIJAtXwcgReiwlC/Thoughtful-Design-System?node-id=4359-55294

### `atoms/glow` — Glow
A blurred copy of the blob, used as light in the background. Usually it sits alone near the bottom of a screen, on its own color cycle; it can also sit right behind a blob and be that blob's own blurred copy. Placement: bottom, behind, or in the flow.
- Props: theme (cool | warm | rose | ember | bloom), cycle (on | off)
- Figma: https://www.figma.com/design/pdq8KcTCIJAtXwcgReiwlC/Thoughtful-Design-System?node-id=4361-70379

### `atoms/wordmark` — Wordmark
The word “thoughtful” as the logotype (an SVG in the current text color, white). 34px tall at lg, 24px at md.
- Props: size (md | lg)
- Figma: https://www.figma.com/design/pdq8KcTCIJAtXwcgReiwlC/Thoughtful-Design-System?node-id=3953-56958

### `atoms/face` — Face

### `atoms/avatar` — Avatar
A round photo, or initials when there's no photo. Use it beside a person's name in lists, cards and headers. Three sizes: 32, 48 and 64.

### `atoms/photo` — Photo

### `atoms/image-placeholder` — Image placeholder

### `atoms/icon` — Icon
A single glyph from the icon library, drawn in the current text color so it works on any surface. Use it inline with text or inside buttons. Three sizes: 16, 24 and 32.
- Props: data-size (sm | md | lg)

### `atoms/icon-button` — Icon button
